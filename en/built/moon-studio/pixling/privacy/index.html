This is the privacy policy for Kkaeal. Kkaeal is a free PC game made by MOON Studio, and you can play it without logging in. Logging in with Google under «Account Linking» in the game unlocks ranked matches, leagues, the board and server saves. This page explains what happens to the information we receive when you do.

⚠ The full policy is everything below «The detailed record starts here». The part above it is a short summary.

The detailed record starts here

MOON Studio (“we”) handles the personal information of people who play the PC game «Kkaeal» as described below. The game can be played without logging in. This policy applies only to players who log in with Google through [Account Linking].

1. What we collect and why

Type What Used for Kept
Required Google unique ID (the account number Google gives us) · in-game nickname · sign-up and last login time · game save · board posts · comments · attached images · date you agreed Login · keeping saves and moving them to another PC · ranked matches · leagues · the board · preventing abuse Until you leave
Optional (only if you turn on «Get news about our next game and events») Email · name · profile photo URL · date you turned it on ① News emails about our next game and events ② how we address you in those emails ③ news matched to your game record Until you turn it off (every 2 years we ask whether you still want it)
Created automatically Access time · a random device number · your IP address converted with a hash key that changes every day (the raw IP is not stored) Counting daily users · preventing sign-up abuse That day only — the next day it is replaced with an anonymous total (daily users) and deleted · the device number of a logged-in player is kept until they leave

You can play the game exactly the same without turning on the optional item. We do not collect birthday, gender, phone number or location.

2. Do we give it to others

We do not sell personal information or give it to other companies, except when the law requires it. We do not put names or photos we receive into advertising. To do that we would ask for separate permission.

3. Who processes it for us and transfers abroad

Recipient What they do Country What is transferred · when · how Kept
Cloudflare, Inc. Servers and data storage (Workers · D1) United States and other Cloudflare data centers The items in table 1 · whenever the game sends them to the server · over encrypted internet connections Same as table 1
Google LLC Confirming Google login United States When you log in, Google gives us your account number (and, if you chose the option, email, name and photo URL) Under Google’s policy

If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can play without logging in, or leave. Without logging in you cannot use ranked matches, leagues, posting on the board or server saves.

4. How to delete it and your rights

  • Turning off news: In the game, Account Linking › [Off]. Your email, name and photo are deleted right away. The [Unsubscribe] link in our emails does the same.
  • Leaving: In the game, Account Linking › [Leave]. Your account, server saves, board posts, comments and images are deleted right away. Ranking and league records stay without a name, as «a trainer who left», only so that the standings still add up. The save on your PC stays.
  • You can also ask to see, correct or stop the processing of your information through the contact below.
  • Images in the bug tab are deleted automatically after 10 days.

5. Children under 14

If you are under 14, please log in only after getting a parent’s or guardian’s consent. «News» can only be turned on by players aged 14 or over. If we learn that information from a child under 14 came in without a guardian’s consent, we delete it right away.

6. How we protect it

  • We talk to the server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Login sessions are stored as hash values, not as the original. We never receive passwords.
  • Operator tools open only with an admin key. We do not write emails or names into logs.

7. Person in charge and contact

  • Privacy officer: MOON Studio operations (moonstudio@aitryed.com)
  • Contact: moonstudio@aitryed.com · Instagram DM @munbanggu100w
  • Reporting a privacy violation (Korea): Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr · 118 in Korea) · Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr · 1833-6972)

8. When this policy changes

If this policy changes, we announce it in the game (patch notes on the board) 7 days before it takes effect, and ask for your consent again at your next login (the consent version changes).
