There are two slots because “more than one game” has to be visible in the structure. Leave a single empty slot and it reads as “the next game” — which still assumes a number.

Part What this slot requires
Design Can the way you win be written in two sentences
Spec Do the rules fit one screen, and is the undecided part written down
Art Has it been decided what won’t be made
QA Can you say in one sentence what a pass means

No Client part here either. A client part attaches after code exists; the four above are what has to exist before it.

The same text appearing twice isn’t laziness — there is no evidence yet that they differ. Write down an invented difference and the first job later is deleting it.
